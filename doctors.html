Doctor consultation FAQs

How do I book a doctor appointment?

Open HealthHome, choose Doctor Consultation, then follow the appointment options shown in the app.

Can I see which doctors and times are available?

Current provider and appointment information is presented in the app and may depend on your location and service availability.

Where can I find my appointment information?

Open the My Appointments area in HealthHome to review the appointment information available to your account.

HealthHome’s website provides service information, not medical advice. For urgent or emergency care, contact your local emergency service.
