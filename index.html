How It Works

A clear path through everyday care

Patients can choose one service at a time. A doctor visit, medicine order and lab test are independent journeys.

  1. 01

    Get HealthHome

    Request the current app link and set up access using the options available in the app.

  2. 02

    Choose a service

    Select doctor consultation, medicine delivery or home lab tests.

  3. 03

    Follow the app flow

    Review the options and details currently shown for your selected service.

  4. 04

    Return to your activity

    Use the appointment, order and report areas available in your HealthHome account.

How a pharmacy conversation can work

  1. 01
    Tell us about your pharmacy

    Share your location and the service you want to explore.

  2. 02

    Confirm applicable business details, service coverage and prescription-handling responsibilities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers, without the guesswork.

For current service availability and account-specific details, check the HealthHome app or contact our team.

What is HealthHome?

HealthHome is a healthcare platform with app areas for doctor consultation, medicine ordering and home lab tests.

How can patients book a doctor appointment?

Open the HealthHome app, choose Doctor Consultation and follow the appointment options currently shown in your account.

How can doctors, pharmacies or laboratories register?

Use the contact form to ask about partnerships. Our team can confirm current onboarding requirements and the service model.

Where can patients find appointment and report information?

The app preview includes appointment, prescription, medicine order and lab report areas. The information available depends on the services and account options currently active in HealthHome.

Can patients track medicine orders?

The HealthHome app includes a medicine orders area. Open the app to review the order information available to your account.

Is online payment available?

Payment options are not listed on this website. Check the current options shown in the app before completing a service request.
