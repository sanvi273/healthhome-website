Questions

Medicine delivery FAQs

How do I place a medicine order?

Open Medicine Delivery in HealthHome, review the items available to you, and follow the order steps shown in the app.

Can I see my existing orders?

The app includes a medicine orders area. Open it to review the current order information available to your account.

Are all medicines and delivery options available everywhere?

Availability depends on the options shown in the app for your location. Check the current details before placing an order.

HealthHome’s website provides service information, not medical advice. Use medicines only as directed by a qualified healthcare professional and follow applicable prescription requirements.
